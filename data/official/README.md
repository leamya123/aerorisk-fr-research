# 2026-10-15 · Official policy rates: first verified comparative panel

## Files
- `ecb_policy_events.csv`: date-effective ECB Deposit Facility Rate (DFR) and Main Refinancing Operations Rate (MRO); rate % p.a. Official ECB rate table (snapshot checked 2026-10-15): https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/key_ecb_interest_rates/html/index.en.html
- `bceao_policy_events.csv`: date-effective BCEAO minimum bid (TMS) and marginal lending rates; rate % p.a. BCEAO **Bulletin trimestriel des statistiques, premier trimestre 2026, page 4, Table 1.1**: https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2026-06/Bulletin-Trimestriel-des-Statistiques-mars-2026.pdf. The 2016-12 starting level is additionally supported by BCEAO **Bulletin mensuel des statistiques, février 2026, policy rate table**: https://bceao.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Bulletin-mensuel-des-statistiques-F%C3%A9vrier-2026.pdf
- `../processed/central_bank_policy_monthly_v1.csv`: derived **end-of-calendar-month** rates, 2020-01 through 2026-09 inclusive. Generated by `python src/build_macro_panel.py` from the official event rows. Monthly sampling = latest effective policy level **at month end**, *not average policy rate over the month*.

## Validation and interpretation
All dates were transcribed from the official publications; source URLs remain in the event records. This is an initial research transcription, **not an official machine-delivered API extract**. Independent second-person review against the source tables is recommended prior to journal submission.

The public ECB rate table identifies the **DFR**, not MRO, as its primary monetary-policy steering rate from March 2024. A change in their interest-rate corridor on 2024-09-18 makes treating MRO and DFR as interchangeable inappropriate.

The first ECB DFR decrease after its 2023 peak takes effect **2024-06-12**. BCEAO did not cut its TMS until **2025-06-16**. This is an observed sequence only; it does **not** establish a lagged *causal response* or prove full monetary-policy transmission, especially because the lending rates, FX regime, banking-sector liquidity and shocks can differ.

The data panel is **not a priced aircraft lease dataset**. No monthly airline credit data, actual lease contracts, Cape Town Convention enforcement case durations, or observed residual-value data have been added. **BEAC is excluded pending an independently verified full time series**; a current-value homepage display is not a historical series.

For the paper, analyze policy-setting transmission descriptively first, then use validated bank lending/interbank outcomes, macro controls, structural-break checks, and appropriate identification before any causal claims. Only use real lease outcomes when their licencing and availability have been confirmed.
